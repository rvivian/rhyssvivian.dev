Identity is one of the places where cybersecurity, infrastructure, user experience, and governance all meet. Good IAM work depends on more than selecting a platform. It depends on careful sequencing, communication, and operational readiness.

My experience includes work across Entra, Okta, and RSA contexts, with emphasis on practical controls and the support model around them.

The details vary by environment, but the theme is consistent: identity systems have to be secure enough to satisfy risk requirements and usable enough that the organization can actually operate.
