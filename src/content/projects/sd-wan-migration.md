Large infrastructure migrations are tests of both architecture and execution. The technical destination matters, but so does the path: dependencies, change windows, fallback plans, site readiness, and stakeholder communication.

The SD-WAN work is a useful example of infrastructure leadership because it required the technical and organizational sides to move together.

Those projects shape how I think about platform work today: better abstractions are only valuable when teams can adopt and operate them.
